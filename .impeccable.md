## Design Context

### Users
Community members and families come first, as confirmed by the owner on October 7, 2026. Players, prospective members, volunteers, and sponsors are supporting audiences. Prioritize understanding CICA, finding approachable ways to participate, and connecting with the local community.

### Brand Personality
Warm, welcoming, community-minded. The owner explicitly chose a community/family priority and requested CICA logo branding. Premium should mean care, clarity, authenticity, and dependable interactions.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the CICA logo as the brand anchor. The owner approved adapting the Growlio reference into a warm editorial community website, with spacious cream layouts, deep blue structure, sage and yellow accents, and readable serif headings. Use the approved CICA, CPL team and sponsor artwork documented in `docs/brand-assets.md`. No community photography was supplied; do not invent people, matches or testimonials. Retain static Namecheap hosting with same-origin PHP forms.

### Design Principles
1. Make joining and understanding participation easy for newcomers.
2. Use verified stories, real photos, and current information.
3. Preserve the logo and reinforce CICA identity consistently.
4. Use restrained purposeful motion with a reduced-motion alternative.
5. Keep mobile access, accessibility, static Namecheap compatibility, and low recurring cost central.
